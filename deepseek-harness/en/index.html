DeepSeek Harness: who owns the agent's runtime semantics?

Reading order.For a first encounter with DSH, follow 01–07. The order reflects runtime dependencies, not a feature catalog: establish the end-to-end turn first, then open Cordis composition and the deeper state boundaries.
