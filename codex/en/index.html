Codex Source Notes

This series reads openai/codex by following one ordinary request: how it becomes a typed operation, enters a session and turn, assembles context, exposes tools, checks permissions, updates clients, writes recovery records, and extracts lessons that later threads can reuse.

The English pages are standalone articles, not route stubs. Source claims are tied to a fixed openai/codex public snapshot; product contracts come from OpenAI documentation.
