AI Engineering · Seven-part route

Stop chasing terms.See the whole system.

Prompt, context, harness, and loop are not successive replacements. They are engineering surfaces of the same agent system, separated by boundary and time scale. This series follows one task through a run, across runs, and into eval-driven system change.
