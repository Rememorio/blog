Source notes · Two system routes

From durable jobs to delivered media

An AI video is not one model call. It is a production that waits, fails, revises, spends money, and eventually gets inspected. Temporal explains how a job survives processes; OpenMontage explains how an agent advances creative work through stages, artifacts, and human approval.

Key distinction: durable execution answers whether the work still exists. Creative orchestration answers what to do next after execution resumes. The ideas are adjacent, but their checkpoints are not interchangeable.
