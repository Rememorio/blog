Source reading · eight-part route

OpenClaw: from “can chat” to “can stay alive”

The hard part is not how a model writes one reply. It is how a message reaches the right Session, when a Tool may run, how child work returns, and which interrupted work can safely continue after restart. These eight chapters follow one source snapshot from a single message through always-on operation and recovery.

How to use the route.Read 01–08 in order for a first tour. For diagnosis, jump directly to routing, context, capabilities, security, or recovery. Every chapter states its prerequisites near the top and keeps source links and official documentation at the end.
